Main Topic

Emotional Intelligence in the Workplace (ESL B2-C1)

Competency & Learning Outcome

Competency: Applies advanced emotional intelligence and professional assertive communication in dynamic corporate marketing contexts.
Outcome: Demonstrates advanced self-regulation and empathetic negotiation through structured corporate roleplays.

Montano Habits & Soft Skills

Seek First to Understand Synergize Advanced ESL Communication

Motivational Introduction (5 minutes)

Activation Task

Opening Prompt (B2-C1 Discussion)

"What makes someone truly effective to work with in a high-pressure corporate ecosystem?"

Encourage advanced ESL students to use idioms or upper-intermediate lexical phrases (e.g., "emotional resilience," "keeping one's composure," "constructive synergy").

Session Objective

Master professional English vocabulary related to emotional intelligence, conflict mitigation, and workplace emotional agility.

Montano Habit in Action

Seek First to Understand, Then to Be Understood: Practicing active listening techniques before expressing critical business opinions.

Individual Work (20 minutes)

Theory & Critical Analysis

1. Emotional Intelligence Pillars (10 min)

Analyze Goleman’s framework applied to modern international marketing environments:

  • Self-Awareness: Identifying personal emotional triggers under deadlines.
  • Self-Regulation: Managing impulsivity in client negotiations.
  • Empathy: Decoding client and coworker unstated needs.
  • Social Skills: Networking and driving collaborative consensus.

2. Guided Written Reflection (10 min)

Reflective prompt: "Describe a past instance when negative feedback triggered a defensive reaction. How could you reframe that communication using C1-level professional phrasing?"

Resources: Notebook, professional vocabulary glossary, and self-regulation rubric.

Expected linguistic outputs for B2-C1 students:

• Students should shift from emotional statements ("I felt attacked") to professional reframing ("I perceived the feedback as an opportunity to recalibrate our marketing strategy").

• Emphasize the use of modal verbs for diplomacy (e.g., "It might be beneficial to...", "We could potentially restructure...").

Cooperative / Teamwork (30 minutes)

Roles & Case Simulation

Pair Roles & Simulation Tasks

Analyze fictional high-pressure corporate communication dilemmas in pairs.

Role 1 (Empathy Analyst): Assesses emotional tone and interpersonal friction.
Role 2 (Conflict Mediator): Drafts assertive English dialogue resolutions.
Montano Habit: Synergize (Synthesizing distinct peer insights into a polished solution).

Concrete Deliverable

Create a structured matrix outlining 2 workplace conflict case studies containing:

  • The communication breakdown / root cause.
  • Impact on team morale and project velocity.
  • Redrafted assertive English response scripts.
Prepare a 2-minute oral presentation using advanced discourse markers for the plenary.
